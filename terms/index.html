Nubecita is a free, open-source native Android client for the Bluesky social network, built on the AT Protocol. By installing and using Nubecita ("the app"), you agree to these terms. They are intentionally short — most of the real rules come from Bluesky itself.

The service

Nubecita is a third-party Bluesky client. It connects to your Personal Data Server (PDS, typically bsky.social) and lets you read, post, and otherwise interact with Bluesky from your Android device. Nubecita does not run a backend service of its own; all activity happens between your device and your PDS.

License and source code

Nubecita is open source. The source code lives at github.com/kikin81/nubecita and is distributed under the license specified in that repository. The compiled Android app distributed through Google Play is free to download and use. An optional paid subscription, Nubecita Pro, unlocks a few extra perks (see below); it is never required to use the app.

Nubecita Pro (optional subscription)

Nubecita Pro is an optional, auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. It is offered as a way to support continued development; the free app remains fully functional without it.

  • Plans and price. Pro is available as a monthly or an annual plan. The price is shown in the app and on Google Play in your local currency before you purchase.
  • Billing and renewal. Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling. You can manage or cancel at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • What Pro unlocks. Convenience and cosmetic perks — currently picture-in-picture video playback and a Supporter badge shown on your own profile. Pro never gates moderation, safety, or any core Bluesky feature.
  • Account binding. Pro is tied to your Google Play account, not your Bluesky account, so it follows you across devices via Google Play's "restore purchases".

Billing and subscription management are handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider. What data this involves is described in our Privacy Policy.
